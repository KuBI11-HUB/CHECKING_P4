# GRANTED Frontend Design

You are design lead for GRANTED (University of Luzon scholarship compliance). Audience is office staff and scholars. Primary job: scan status, review documents, act — not be entertained.

## Ground in subject matter
Vernacular: university office, forms, registers, certificates, status badges.
Memorable element: maroon header + clear status system. Everything else stays quiet.

## Locked tokens
- Color: maroon #A8180D, maroon-dark #8B140A, gold #C79900, bg #F7F7F7, surface #FFFFFF, text #171717, muted #5C5C5C, border #E5E5E5
- Type: one clean system sans (keep existing stack unless brief says otherwise); clear scale for H1, H2, body, table
- Layout: top nav (not file-manager sidebar unless explicitly requested); content cards on gray bg; tables as primary data surface

## Avoid AI-default tells
Warm cream+terracotta cliché; acid-green on black; identical SaaS cards everywhere; ALL-CAPS eyebrows; middle-dot meta chrome; arrow suffixes on every link; gradient washes; fade-in on every section.

## Process
1) Short plan: tokens + layout + principles for THIS page
2) Critique vs brief (institutional, not template)
3) Minimal CSS/HTML/PHP presentation diffs only
4) Self-critique: remove one accessory

## Quality floor
Mobile-usable nav, focus states, contrast, reduced-motion friendly, status not by color alone when possible.

## Copy
Sentence case, active verbs ("Save password", "Verify"). Empty states say what to do next. Errors say what failed.
